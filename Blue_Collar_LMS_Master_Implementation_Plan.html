2. 4-Tier Skill Level Taxonomy & Milestone Progression

Modules and workers are categorized into four standardized skill tiers to track career advancement from helper to master technician:

Skill Tier Target Workers Pedagogical Format Verification Milestone
1. Unskilled Helpers, Loaders, Cleaners, Farm Labourers 90-second visual animations with native audio voiceover; zero reading requirement. Bronze Milestone: "Spot the Hazard" tap quiz + PPE verification.
2. Semi-Skilled Assembly Line, Delivery Agents, Machine Tenders 2–3 min step-by-step SOP videos and interactive tool checklists. Silver Milestone: Swipe-card decision tests + tool upkeep check.
3. Skilled Electricians, Welders, Masons, Plumbers, Cooks 3–5 min technical procedures, circuit schematics, recipe proportions. Gold Milestone: Practical Photo Proof upload + supervisor sign-off.
4. Highly-Skilled Quality Inspectors, CNC Programmers, HVAC Diagnosticians Precision calibration, defect troubleshooting, flowchart diagnostics. Master Milestone: Audio verbal explanation + diagnostic exam.

3. Solving Gaps Faced by iGOT Karmayogi & Legacy Corporate LMSs

The Problem with Existing Platforms (e.g. iGOT Karmayogi): Government and enterprise LMS platforms consistently suffer 70%–85% drop-off among frontline personnel. They feature 40-page PDF policy circulars, desktop-oriented interfaces, complex email/password gates, theoretical multiple-choice quizzes that anyone can guess, and zero vernacular audio for low-literacy workers.
Critical Dimension iGOT Karmayogi / Standard Corporate LMS Our Frontline LMS Innovation
Device Usability Desktop/Laptop biased; clumsy layout on low-end phones. Mobile-First PWA: Touch targets ≥ 48px; runs smoothly on budget 2GB RAM phones.
Literacy & Audio Dense text circulars; English or formal Hindi only. Dual-Track Voiceover: "Tap to Listen" in 11 native Indian dialects.
Authentication Email + Password + Captcha (massive barrier). Zero-Email: Phone OTP (WhatsApp/SMS) or 1-second Camera QR badge scan.
Connectivity Fails in basements, rural fields, or remote sites. True Offline Sync: IndexedDB local caching with background queue sync.
Practical Proof Theoretical MCQs only. Hands-On Assignments: Photo proof uploads & recorded voice explanations.
Group Floor Mode Solo user accounts only. Supervisor "Toolbox Talk" Mode: Group video stream with 1-click crew attendance.

5. Hands-on Practical Assignments Engine

📸 Photo Proof Assignment

Worker photographs their completed physical task (e.g. welded joint, tile alignment, wire insulation) and submits for supervisor review.

🎙️ Audio Voice Assignment

Low-literacy workers tap "Record Audio" and speak for 30–60 seconds in their native dialect explaining a safety hazard or troubleshooting step.

🎯 Visual Tap Assessment

Workers tap directly on 3 safety hazards in a high-res worksite photograph or sequence tools in order of operation.

6. Admin Capabilities: Dual-View Tracking & Module Authoring

  • Category-Wise Tracking: High-level view showing training completion and compliance percentages across all 7 sectors and 4 skill tiers.
  • Person-Wise Tracking: Detailed drill-down into each individual worker’s profile showing modules passed, assignment photo submissions, scores, and active certifications.
  • Skill-Based Module Upload Builder: Admin uploads video lessons, assigns target sector, designates skill tier (Unskilled to Highly-Skilled), and links 11-language audio voiceovers.
  • Printable QR Code Generator: Generates equipment QR stickers and worker ID login badges.
  • OSHA & Labor Inspection Export: One-click PDF/CSV audit reports with digital timestamps and signatures.
