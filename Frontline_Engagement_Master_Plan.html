1. The Hybrid Design: Coursera/Udemy Exterior + YouTube Shorts Interior

To attract frontline laborers and eliminate drop-off, the application bridges two world-class paradigms:

🎓 Coursera & Udemy Style Portal

Professional, organized structure for courses, certifications, and career growth:

  • 7-Sector Course Catalog: Construction, Technical, Manufacturing, Logistics, Agriculture, Cleaning, Hospitality.
  • Syllabus & Lesson Drawer: Clean chapter breakdown, progress tracking, and downloadable checklists.
  • Wage & Milestone Incentives: Direct visibility into earning potential (e.g. "Unlock Tier 2 → +₹1,500/mo allowance").
  • Verifiable Digital Skill Passport: Portable, official QR-credential for career progression.

📱 YouTube Shorts / Reels Microlearning Engine

Addictive, bite-sized vertical video feed tailored for mobile workers:

  • 9:16 Vertical Video Snap-Feed: Workers swipe up/down through 60-second micro-lessons.
  • Floating Action Bar: Instant 30-sec quiz button, ask-supervisor voice note, like counter.
  • Split-Screen Badging: High-contrast Wrong Way ❌ vs. Right Way ✔️ overlays.
  • 11 Indian Native Languages: Persistent audio switcher ("Tap to Listen") with native dialects.

4. Hands-on Practical Field Assignments Engine

📸 Photo Proof Assignment

Worker captures a photo of physical work (welded joint, wire termination, tile alignment). Automatically compressed on-device (<500 KB) and pushed to supervisor review queue.

🎙️ Audio Voice Response

Low-literacy workers tap "Record Audio" and speak for 30–60 seconds in their native tongue explaining emergency cutoff or machine pre-check steps.

🎯 Spot the Hazard Touch Game

Interactive image canvas where workers tap directly on 3 safety hazards in a high-resolution photograph with instant audio feedback.

5. Admin & Supervisor Management Tools

  • Category-Wise & Person-Wise Analytics: Admin can view macro health by sector (e.g. Construction: 84% certified) or drill down to a single worker's profile (Ramesh Kumar: 4 modules passed, Tier 2 unlocked).
  • Admin Shorts & Course Studio: Upload 9:16 vertical videos, attach audio tracks across 11 Indian languages, configure quiz coordinates, and set skill tier.
  • Supervisor Shift Tablet Mode: Morning Toolbox Talk player with 1-click group attendance confirmation and practical observation signature canvas.
  • 1-Click OSHA / Labor Audit Export: Downloadable, tamper-proof PDF audit dossier containing verified completion timestamps and digital signatures.
