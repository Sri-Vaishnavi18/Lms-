2. Screen-by-Screen Visual Architecture

Exact visual paradigms replicated from Coursera, Udemy, and YouTube Shorts.

📚
Coursera & Udemy Exterior
Web & Tablet Portal (/courses & /courses/:id)
  • Coursera Mega-Navigation: "Explore Catalog" classified by all 7 frontline sectors.
  • Udemy Course Cards: 16:9 thumbnail, Bestseller tag, instructor credentials, star ratings (4.9 ★), and enrolled worker counts.
  • Wage Incentive Badges: Every card highlights the exact monthly wage differential unlocked upon tier completion.
  • Sticky Syllabus Drawer: Collapsible chapter accordion showing lesson checkmarks, durations, and progress meters.
  • Practical Assignment Tab: Workers upload site photos and record mother-tongue voice notes.
⚡
YouTube Shorts Vertical Engine
Mobile & JIT Machine SOP (/shorts)
  • 9:16 Vertical Snap-Scroll: Seamless gesture swiping up/down between bite-sized 45–60s micro-lessons.
  • Right-Hand Action Column: Thumbs Up 👍, Dislike 👎, Shift Foreman Walkie-Talkie 💬, 30-Sec Quiz 🎯, Offline Download 📥, Share QR ↗️.
  • Spinning Audio Vinyl Disc: Displays active native Indian language with real-time speech narration wave.
  • Split-Screen Badging: Interactive [❌ WRONG WAY] vs. [✔️ RIGHT WAY] safety alert buttons.
  • Machine QR Trigger: Scanning physical machine sticker launches its vertical Short directly.

4. Four-Tier Skill Progression & Wage Ladders

Real-world career advancement with verified digital skill passport credentials.

Skill Tier Curriculum Focus Verification Method Wage & Career Benefit
Tier 1: Unskilled Basic site induction, 100% PPE compliance, zero energy awareness Spot-the-Hazard Touch Quiz Entry-level clearance to enter project jobsite
Tier 2: Semi-Skilled Equipment pre-shift SOPs, forklift reverse checks, machine guards Visual Quiz + 1 Practical Photo +₹1,200 to ₹1,500/month machine allowance
Tier 3: Skilled Lockout-Tagout (LOTO), arc flash PPE, complex machinery operations Field Photo Proof + Voice Note +₹2,000 to ₹2,500/month autonomous operator grade
Tier 4: Highly-Skilled Toolbox talk coaching, substation diagnostics, emergency response Supervisor In-Person Signature +₹3,200/month Shift Foreman / Lead Inspector status
